FAQ

Questions we get
all the time.

How far in advance do I need to order? +
We ask for at least 48 hours notice for small orders (under 12 loaves). Larger events or weddings should book 1–2 weeks in advance to secure your date.
What is the minimum order for catering? +
Our minimum catering order is 6 loaves. For events, we recommend calculating roughly 1 loaf per 6–8 guests for an appetizer or 1 loaf per 3–4 guests if bread is a centerpiece.
Do you deliver to the whole Denver metro? +
Yes. We deliver throughout the Denver metro area including Thornton, Westminster, Arvada, Aurora, Denver, Lakewood, and surrounding communities. Delivery fee depends on distance.
Can I get a custom flavor for my event? +
Absolutely. Custom flavors are one of our favorite things to do. Tell us what you’re thinking — flavor profile, occasion, dietary needs — and we’ll create something specifically for your event.
Is the bread baked the day of the event? +
Always. We do not pre-bake catering orders. Your bread is made fresh the morning of your event so your guests experience it at its absolute peak.
Do you offer gluten-free options? +
We have a small selection of gluten-free recipes. Contact us to discuss what’s available and whether cross-contamination is a concern for your guests.
