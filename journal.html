The Journal

Travel. Ingredients. Bread stories.

Notes from the road, the kitchen, and everywhere in between — where we find inspiration, and how it ends up in a loaf.

Currently Documenting

We don't just get inspired by the world.
We go there.

Urban Bread Co. is actively planning a trip to Mexico — Cabo and beyond — to source ingredients and inspiration in person: local markets, family bakeries, street vendors, and the flavors that don't translate over a phone call.

This page is where that trip will live once it happens — photos, videos, ingredients discovered, the bakeries and markets we visit, and the breads that come out of it. Consider this the first entry in an ongoing travelogue.
