The Origin

Bread that was
never supposed to exist.

Urban Bread Co started with a simple question: why does artisan bread have to look and taste like every other artisan bread? The flavors we grew up on — the bold, the unexpected, the soulful — weren’t showing up in any bakery we walked into.

So we started making them ourselves. A kitchen, a vision, and a refusal to be ordinary. What began as experiments became 82+ recipes, each one with a name, a story, and a reason for existing.

Hip hop taught us that culture shapes everything. The beats, the bars, the attitude. Urban Bread Co brings that same energy to bread — every loaf is made with intention, creativity, and respect for the craft.

How We Bake

The process
behind every loaf.

1
Start with the concept

Every loaf starts with an idea — a flavor pairing, a cultural reference, a name that deserves a bread. We build the recipe from there.

2
Source the right ingredients

No shortcuts. We find the best version of every ingredient. The flour, the add-ins, the toppings — all chosen deliberately.

3
Mix, rest, develop

Good bread takes time. We don’t rush the fermentation, the proofing, or the development. The dough tells us when it’s ready.

4
Bake to order

Your loaf is baked fresh when you order it. No sitting in a case for three days. You get it at its best.

5
Deliver & repeat

Pickup or delivery in the Denver metro area. Then we go back to the kitchen and start on the next one.
