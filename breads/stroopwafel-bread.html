Stroopwafel Bread

Caramel waffle cookie inspired — built to taste like the warm, sticky-sweet stroopwafels sold from carts across the Netherlands.

Flavor Profile
Caramel • Cinnamon • Spiced • Chewy
The Story

Stroopwafels are the Netherlands' favorite street snack — thin waffle cookies filled with a syrupy caramel center, traditionally warmed by resting one over a hot cup of coffee. This loaf takes that same caramel-and-spice character and bakes it into a full, shareable bread.

The Ingredients

Stroopwafel syrup, brown sugar, cinnamon, butter caramel glaze.
