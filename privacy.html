Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Urban Bread Co ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit urbanbreadco.com, place an order, join Bread Club, or contact us, and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Order & contact details — name, email, phone number, delivery address, and order contents when you place an order or fill out a form.
  • Payment information — processed securely by our third-party payment and form provider; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage data — pages visited, browser type, and general analytics to help us understand how people use the site.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to fulfill orders, communicate about deliveries or events, respond to inquiries, and improve the site. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to power parts of this site, including order forms and payment processing (LeadConnector/GoHighLevel) and embedded video (YouTube). These providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We use minimal local storage and cookies to remember your cart and game progress on this site. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email we send.

Children's Privacy

Our site and services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach out at order@urbanbreadco.com or 720-266-8231.
